My Interest: Reading!

As I mentioned in my introduction, one of my favorite hobbies include reading, both fiction and non-fiction books. I have grown up always loving reading, as my mom would always take my sisters and I to our local library for story time. As a teenager, I volunteered at my local library for three summers in a row. I would reshelve books and DVDs, set up events like craft time or story time for children, and help patrons with technology and other requests. I loved it because it was very relaxing, especially when reshelving books!

Last year, I read 43 books. I use an app to track all of the books that I read called StoryGraph. I love it as a data science major because it gives lots of data, similar to Spotify Wrapped. My top genres were literary, thriller, classics, contemporary, and horror. My top authors were Franz Kafka and Iain Reid. On average, I gave books a 3.83 star rating. Some of my 5-star rated books include At Night All Blood is Black by David Diop, The Other Boleyn Girl by Phillipa Gregory, and I’m Thinking of Ending Things by Iain Reid. I hope to read more this year, but sometimes I find it hard to make time for it with schoolwork and everything else going on in life. During the summer, I read a lot more. I love to read at the beach.
