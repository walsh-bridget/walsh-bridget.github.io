About Me

Hi! My name is Bridget. I am a third year student studying Data Science and Mathematics major at Northeastern, originally from Trumbull, Connecticut.

At Northeastern, I am president of the Association for Women in Mathematics, which is a club for women interested in math or related fields. We have networking events, professor talks, trivia nights, and more! I have also worked as a College of Science peer advisor, helping freshmen is COS adjust to college by visiting their classes, providing resources, and hosting drop in hours. I have also been a volunteer and private math tutor for over 7 years, and I love help people learn math!

Some of my hobbies include running, reading, going for walks around Boston, trying new coffeeshops, and listening to music! I love to run or go for walks along the Esplanade, especially during the fall because it is so beautiful. Some of my favorite musical artists include Lorde, Phoebe Bridgers, Clairo, The Strokes, Radiohead, Tame Impala, and Fontaines D.C. A few of my favorite books include Jane Eyre by Charlotte Brontë, The Secret History by Donna Tartt, and If We Were Villains by M.L. Rio. I also love reading non-fiction books about many different topics.
